# Mapperatorinpainter v1.0.1

## Changes

- Year and mapper-ID conditioning can be left blank in both Generate and
  Inpaint. Blank values are passed to Mapperatorinator as `None`, including
  after Generate → Inpaint handoff.
- Lightweight preview playback now keeps a stable audio decoder and uses the
  media element as its sole playback clock. Regenerating, undoing, or redoing a
  map revision reloads the parsed scene without reloading unchanged audio.
- Stale audio-load completions are ignored when the selected source changes.
- Timeline scrubbing pauses playback, previews the cursor visually during the
  drag, and commits one decoder seek on release before resuming. This avoids
  accumulating asynchronous media seeks during a drag.
- The travelling slider ball is rendered above repeat arrows and repeat-count
  badges with a stronger outline and shadow.

## Manual release checks

- Start with blank Year and Mapper ID in Generate and Inpaint, and verify the
  resulting inference configuration leaves both fields unconditioned.
- While preview audio is playing, regenerate, undo, redo, and switch
  difficulties; verify playback resumes at the intended timestamp without
  accumulating an offset.
- Scrub repeatedly before and after a revision change, including while paused.
- Scrub long distances while playing and confirm playback resumes only after
  the map and audio have landed on the same timestamp.
- Preview sliders with one and several repeats and confirm the ball stays
  visible at both endpoints.
